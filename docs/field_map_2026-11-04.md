# Field map: the fifty physician credentialing fields

Filed 2026-11-04, written 2026-11-05, citations read 2026-11-05. The fields a health plan or hospital collects to credential a physician, grouped as a credentialing file is. For each one: the standard and element that ask for it, the public document that says so (by key; see Sources read), the primary source, how often that source changes or must be re-read, and where the garden stands on it today.

**How the citations work.** Every standard in the table was read on 2026-11-05 in a public document listed under Sources read, cited by key and page. Where the element or factor number is only in a paid manual (the NCQA standards and guidelines, the Joint Commission CAMH/CAMCAH), the family is cited and the number carries **[CONFIRM]**. A standard no public document read today names is marked "[CONFIRM]: not in the documents read".

**NCQA numbering.** The 2025 HPA standards, as NCQA's own 17 Nov 2025 corrections (N2) cite them, keep CR 3 as three elements: A, Verification of Credentials, in six factors; B, Sanction Information; C, Credentialing Application. The public-comment redline (N1, November 2023, marked "Obsolete After January 15, 2024") proposed splitting CR 3 into Elements A to H; N2 shows that split was not adopted. N1 is cited for factor wording that N2 does not repeat. Where N1's redline shows two time limits side by side and N2 does not settle which one stands, the limit carries [CONFIRM].

`freshroot/fields.json` is generated from this file by `docs/build_fields_json.py`. Edit the tables, then run the script; `--check` fails when the JSON is out of date.

## NCQA CR 5: ongoing monitoring

NCQA's 2025 HPA standards (as corrected in N2, pp.13 to 15) require the organization to collect and review, between recredentialing cycles, Medicare and Medicaid sanctions (CR 5 Element A, factor 1) and Medicare and Medicaid exclusions (factor 2), with sanctions, limitations and expiration on licensure reviewed on the same clock. The time frame for reviewing sanction, exclusion, limitation and expiration information now reads "At least every 30 calendar days" (N2 p.15, clarification released 7/28/25). Adverse events (factor 5) are monitored at least every 6 months before July 1, 2025 and at least monthly on or after it (N2 p.15). For factors 1 to 3, an organization that uses a monitoring service shows NCQA "evidence of the subscription with the service" (N2 p.13, scope of review). Sanction and exclusion information is verified for all product lines (N2 p.13). Accepted sources: for Medicaid sanctions, any of the state Medicaid agency, the AMA Physician Masterfile, FSMB, NPDB or SAM.gov; for Medicaid exclusions, any of the state Medicaid agency, the OIG LEIE or NPDB (N2 p.3, policy change 11/17/25); for Medicare exclusions, the Medicare Exclusion Database, the OIG LEIE or NPDB (N2 p.14). The same changes apply to Credentialing Accreditation as CRA 5 (N3 pp.10 and 12). That factor 3 is the licensure factor is inferred from "factors 1-3" and the time-frame heading, and carries [CONFIRM]. FreshRoot's deltas feed is an input to this review, not the review itself.

## Coverage classes

| Class | Meaning |
|---|---|
| covered today | A live, polled source in the garden holds this field, and the FreshRoot API serves it: as a receipt with its three clocks, or as a provider fact from NPPES. |
| public file | A publisher releases it as a file anyone can get. Not loaded yet. The detail names the file. |
| agreement needed | The primary source sells, licenses or restricts it. The detail names the agreement. |
| self-reported | Only the physician can supply it, on the application or attestation. A primary source may confirm it one record at a time, but no bulk file exists. |
| plan-internal | The credentialing organization makes it: decisions, dates, reviews. |

Headline groups, in the `Drives` column:

- **Expired**: a date or status that lapses, so a credential can be out of date while nothing else changed.
- **Sanctioned**: a finding by a board, a payer or a registry that bars or limits practice or billing.
- **Boarded**: what says the physician is board certified, in what, and whether it is current.

## Reading the counts

13 fields are covered today, 8 sit in public files not loaded yet, 13 need an agreement, 14 only the physician can supply, and 2 belong to the plan. The public files are the cheapest next coverage: board actions (F12) are already loaded by the TX, FL and CA board loaders and need only a receipt writer, and the four CMS files (F26, F29, F41, F43) come from the same catalogs as the opt-out and PECOS loaders, on monthly, every-3.5-day and scheduled cadences (C3, C4).
