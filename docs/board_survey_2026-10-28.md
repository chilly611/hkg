# US physician licensing boards: licensee data access survey

Survey date 2026-10-27 (TX, FL, CA and UT read 2026-10-28), filed 2026-10-28. 50 states plus DC. One row per board: MD boards, separate DO boards where they exist, and one row where one board licenses both.

## Summary

63 boards in 51 jurisdictions.

- **Download: 27.** A self-serve file or open-data set; some require an account.
- **Request: 2.** A request form or email, with no fee stated.
- **Purchase: 24.** A fee is stated, even when the amount is not shown.
- **Lookup only: 2.** Official pages show no bulk file, only single lookup.
- **Unknown: 8.** No official page says either way.

- Both lookup-only boards (OK MD board, WI) had a list service that is now suspended.
- No board file confirmed here carries an NPI. Matching runs through license number or name, as in `boards/common.py`.
- TX, FL, CA and UT, where the repo already has loaders or source rows, were read from official pages. Each disagreement with the repo is in that row's Notes under "Repo vs official": Florida's cadence is daily (the `data_sources` seed says monthly), Utah's list covers DOs and has per-record fees, and California's DCA drop does carry an osteopathic file.
- The lookup URLs in `seed_state_boards.py` (on main: `scripts/ingestion/archive/seed_state_boards.py`) are unverified and none matches the official lookup URL found here, so none were reused.

## Method

- Read only official board or state pages: board sites, state `.gov` pages, state open-data portals, and statutes and rules.
- Lookup pages were loaded at most once to confirm the URL. No searches were submitted, no bulk files were downloaded and no forms were sent.
- "—" means no official page confirmed the value. "Channel", "Cost", "Cadence" and "Terms" in Notes carry qualifiers moved out of the short cells.
- Bucket rule: a row with a free download and a paid option counts as download (DE, RI, TX). A request route that states a fee counts as purchase (WY, MN, MT, NE, UT).
- Spot-checked first-hand after the survey: NC (price, cadence, terms), GA (prices, fields), WA (dataset publisher, license, cadence, physician rows), MD (CSV rosters, monthly), OH (monthly roster) and IL (dataset owner, ODbL, "MEDICAL BOARD" rows).

## Open questions

- **Unknown, worth a records request:** DC, MA, ME (Board of Licensure in Medicine), NV DO board, NY, ND, SD, WV DO board.
- **Maine:** both boards fold into a new Maine Board of Medicine (32 MRSA ch. 153) on 2027-01-01, per statute pages. Re-survey Maine after that date.
- **New Jersey:** the free standard lists and the $55 custom list come only from a search-engine copy of DCA's "Request a Licensee List" page. The page is behind a bot check, which was not bypassed.
- **Price not published:** AZ MD board (statute caps the CD at $100), MN, MT and NE.
- **DO coverage not seen in the data:** WA's DO coverage rests on DOH's description of the dataset; no osteopathic credential type appeared in its metadata. CT and IL physician rows are inferred from dataset descriptions and metadata, not queried.
- **Terms of reuse:** only AZ DO, MS, NC, SC, NM, OR, UT and the open-data sets (CO, CT, DE, IL, WA) give terms or a license at a URL. HI and IN show terms inside their purchase flows, with no URL of their own. UT's list falls under R156-1-106 (no disclosure, no solicitation), quoted on a DOPL form. TX ORSSP and MBC's database use accept-by-checkbox terms whose text is not published, and the TX open-data set has no license. Everywhere else, including FL and the CA DCA drop, the reuse terms are unknown.
